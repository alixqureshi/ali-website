Questions

What does “AI-native” actually mean for my team?

Which option is right for us?

That’s what the call is for. As a rough guide: training if your team uses AI but inconsistently, an install if you want your business set up with a first agent running, and bespoke builds if you want systems built around how you work.

How much does it cost?

It depends on your team and the scope. You’ll know the fee on the call, before you decide anything.

Do we need to be technical?

No. Your team works in the AI apps you already pay for, and I handle the build.

Is our data safe?

Everything lives in your own accounts, as files you own. Agents draft, and a person approves anything before it goes out.

How long until something is working?

Around 2 weeks for an install, from kick-off to your first agent running. Bespoke builds ship in stages, with something live in the first month.

What happens after you’ve built it?

You own everything, so you can keep going without me. Installs include 14 days of fixes, and bespoke builds are month to month.
