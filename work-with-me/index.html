What we measure

We choose the measures before the build.

SpeedTime from a request to a finished job, including time spent waiting for information or approvals.
CapacityHow much useful work your team can complete, and how much still needs manual fixing or chasing.
CostThe cost of completing the work, including people, my fee and any extra tools.
PerformanceThe result the work serves, such as conversion, customer response time or fewer errors. We choose a measure that fits the problem.

Time saved creates capacity. We count it as a cash saving only when a cost is actually removed.
