Privacy policy.

LAST UPDATED 4 NOVEMBER 2026

AI with Ali is run by ADCRATE LTD, a company registered in England and Wales (number 14492687), registered office 133 Monksfield Way, Slough, England, SL2 1QJ. We decide how the personal data on this site is used. Questions or requests go to ali@adcrate.co.

What we collect

  • Workshop bookings. Stripe takes your payment and gives us your name, email, billing address and what you paid. We never see your full card number.
  • Booking emails. We add your email to Kit to send your join link, reminders, recording and files.
  • Newsletter. If you sign up, Kit holds your email so we can send it. You can unsubscribe from any email.
  • Enquiries. The contact form sends your name, email, company, role, what you tell us and how you reached the site to a database in Notion, through a Cloudflare service.
  • The live session. It runs on Google Meet and is recorded. Your name may be visible to the host, and any question you submit may be read out. Attendee microphones and cameras are off by default.
  • Site visits. Our host, GitHub Pages, may log technical data such as your IP address.

Cookies and advertising

Most pages use the Meta Pixel and Google Analytics. They set cookies (such as _fbp, _fbc and _ga) and record page views, checkout starts, enquiries and newsletter signups. We use this to measure the site and our ads.

When you buy a ticket, our server tells Meta about the purchase so we can measure our ads. That includes the amount, your browser identifiers, and a scrambled (hashed) version of your email, name, location and any phone number you gave Stripe. We may also upload hashed email lists to Meta so that people who shouldn’t see our ads don’t.

If your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, these pages don’t load the pixel or Google Analytics, and we don’t send Meta your purchase.

Why we use it

  • To deliver what you bought (contract).
  • To answer enquiries, run the business, measure ads and keep the site secure (legitimate interests).
  • To send the newsletter, where you asked for it (consent).
  • To keep tax and accounting records (legal obligation).

We don’t sell your data. Buying a ticket doesn’t sign you up to marketing offers.

Who else handles it

Stripe, Kit, Google, Meta, Notion, Cloudflare and GitHub process data for the purposes above. Some are based outside the UK, including in the US, so your data may be transferred there under their standard safeguards.

How long we keep it

We keep booking and financial records for as long as the law requires, usually 6 years. We keep enquiries and session recordings for as long as they’re useful, and newsletter details until you unsubscribe.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to see, correct, delete or move your data, and object to or limit how we use it. Email ali@adcrate.co. If you’re unhappy with how we handle it, you can complain to your local regulator. In the UK, that’s the Information Commissioner’s Office.
