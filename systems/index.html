Two systems we built for Adcrate and run every day. Everything I sell, I’ve used here first.

SYSTEM 01 · THE CREATIVE APP

AdcreateOS · Creative app

adcreateos.com

The app our 50-person team makes ads in. Every brand’s context lives in one place, so briefs, scripts and statics start from what we already know about the brand.

WHAT IT DOES

  • Brand context

    Each brand’s products, voice and past winners in one place.

  • Static ad generator

    The team makes static ads from the brand’s context, start to finish, in one dashboard.

  • Read-only Meta audit

    Pulls ad performance daily and never changes anything in the ad account.

  • Deliver

    Finished ads go to the client with a tag, so live results trace back to the brief.

  • Client view

    Clients see results in a share portal. They can look, not edit.

SYSTEM 02 · THE AGENTS DASHBOARD

AdcreateOS · Agents dashboard

os.adcrate.com

One view of the whole business, from finance and HR to client accounts, performance and ads, plus what every agent is doing.

WHAT IT DOES

  • The whole business in one view

    Finance, HR, client accounts, performance and ads, side by side.

  • One agent per job

    Churn Radar, Creator Comms, Casting, Creator Asks and more.

  • Organised by department

    Creator, Performance, Strategy, Editing, Account and Ops.

  • A person approves

    Agents draft and propose. Replies wait in Slack for a yes.

  • Shadow first

    New agents run alongside the team for a week before we switch them on.
